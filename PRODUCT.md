# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

English-speaking first-time travelers to China. Two modes:

- **Pre-trip researchers** (2-4 weeks before departure): searching "how to pay in China" on Reddit and YouTube, anxious about the digital ecosystem but excited. They want clear, honest, up-to-date answers from someone who actually lives there.
- **On-the-ground reference** (already in China): hit a wall with Alipay verification or can't figure out the metro gate. Pull up the relevant guide on mobile and follow screenshots step by step.

Both modes converge on one job: **get a specific travel logistics problem solved without wading through SEO fluff or outdated forum threads.**

## Product Purpose

China Travel OS exists because China's digital ecosystem — payments, internet, transport, apps — is genuinely disorienting for foreigners, and the existing information landscape is either outdated forum posts, overly polite official guides, or AI-generated listicles that have never actually used Alipay.

Each guide solves one problem completely: real screenshots, real edge cases, real mistakes the author has watched friends make. The site earns trust by being the thing it claims to be — firsthand knowledge from a local who has helped actual travelers through these exact situations.

Success: a traveler lands in China, opens the relevant guide, follows it, and the thing works. They don't need to open five tabs.

## Brand Personality

**Honest / Insider / Warm**

- **Honest**: doesn't sugarcoat. Admits when something is annoying. Tells you the 3% foreign card fee exists before you discover it yourself. No "China is amazing at everything" propaganda.
- **Insider**: written by someone who lives in Hangzhou and has watched friends struggle. The voice of "I've seen this go wrong, here's how to avoid it" rather than "here are the official instructions."
- **Warm**: approachable, human, occasionally funny. Like a friend who's been there. Not cold institutional authority, not hyper-casual bro marketing.

Voice rules: TikTok hooks (personal, visual openings) + Reddit sincerity (accurate, specific, no美化). First-person local experience. Third-person for traveler stories ("a traveler I know").

## Anti-references

- **Official tourism bureau style**: red lanterns, Chinese cultural motifs, gold accents, ornate decoration. This site is modern, international, typography-driven.
- **SaaS product landing pages**: gradient buttons, hero-metric templates (big numbers + small labels), "streamline/empower/supercharge" vocabulary.
- **AI-generated marketing slop**: aphoristic negation cadence ("No fluff. Just results."), eyebrow labels on every section, identical card grids, em-dash overuse.
- **Lonely Planet / WikiTravel density**: encyclopedic information architecture with nested categories and cross-references. This site solves one problem per page, completely.

## Design Principles

1. **Firsthand earns trust.** Every screenshot is real. Every edge case happened to someone. Accuracy and specificity are the brand's credibility — guessing destroys it.
2. **One problem, one page.** Each guide solves a single travel pain point completely. The reader opens the page, follows it, and the thing works. No multi-tab research required.
3. **Voice carries the warmth, not the background color.** Personality comes from honesty and lived detail, not from cream/sand body backgrounds or decorative warmth. The design stays chroma-neutral; the copy brings the human.
4. **Mobile-first as respect.** Half the readers are already in China, on a phone, possibly struggling with VPN. The page must load fast, scroll easily, and show screenshots at readable sizes on a 375px viewport.
5. **Earn the scroll, then get out of the way.** No decorative flourishes between the reader and the answer. Every element justifies its presence by helping the reader solve the problem faster.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG AA (contrast ≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large text)
- Keyboard navigable, screen-reader friendly
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected globally
- Mobile-first responsive design (375px+)
- Screenshots include descriptive alt text
